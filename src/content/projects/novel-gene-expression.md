I adapted AffinityVAE, a variational autoencoder (VAE) originally designed at the Alan Turing Institute, to handle single-cell RNA sequencing (scRNA-seq) data. The adapted model learns a representation of each cell and can generate novel transcriptomic profiles, which could inform cell engineering experiments.

Synthesised profiles like these can also support hypothesis generation and help address data scarcity in genomics research.

This work is not yet published. The original AffinityVAE model is described in [the AffinityVAE paper](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-031-91721-9_12).
