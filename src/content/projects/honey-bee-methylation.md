Investigation of the role of DNA methylation in the behavioural plasticity and development of honey bees. This project explores how environmental factors influence epigenetic marks and subsequently affect gene expression and phenotype.

During a bioinformatics internship at the University of Leicester, I preprocessed honey bee genomic and methylomic sequencing data on a high-performance computing (HPC) cluster for the researchers analysing honey bee traits.
